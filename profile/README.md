## Make your Kindle yours

- **Primitive Launcher** gives you an app grid, wallpapers, battery and display controls, and a way back to the Kindle interface. [Launcher](https://github.com/DoanNguyenTrong/kindle-primitive-launcher)
- **Store** installs and updates apps over Wi-Fi. Explore productivity tools, study apps, and E Ink games. [Store](https://github.com/DoanNguyenTrong/kindle-primitive-store) · [App directory](https://github.com/DoanNguyenTrong/kindle-primitive#the-apps)
- **Primitive Browser** brings paged web reading and night mode to E Ink. **Files** helps you organize documents and open supported books and PDFs in KOReader. [Browser](https://github.com/DoanNguyenTrong/kindle-primitive-browser) · [Files](https://github.com/DoanNguyenTrong/kindle-primitive-files)

## Start on your Kindle

Primitive Launcher is verified on a jailbroken Kindle Oasis 3 running firmware 5.18.2. Install Launcher using
its guide, then open **Store** from the Launcher grid for apps and updates. [Install guide](https://github.com/DoanNguyenTrong/kindle-primitive-launcher/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) · [Launcher releases](https://github.com/DoanNguyenTrong/kindle-primitive-launcher/releases/latest)

## Build for PrimitiveOS
